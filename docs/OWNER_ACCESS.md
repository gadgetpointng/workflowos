# WorkflowOS owner access

The authorized WorkflowOS owner email is `gadgetpoint.ng@gmail.com`.

Until the GadgetPoint Site owner redirect is updated, the owner can use the passwordless WorkflowOS owner-access fallback. The fallback sends a one-time secure sign-in link only to the authorized owner email and does not change staff authentication.

Staff continue to authenticate through GadgetPoint Admin and should never enter their GadgetPoint password directly into WorkflowOS.
